# Paper protocol and numerical definitions

- Density: shifted-Gaussian scenario A, n=1,000, epsilon 0, .1, .2, .3, .4; gamma .1, .5, 1; 30 replicates per condition. Gaussian diagonal covariance with free mean and scales.
- Synthetic regression: 1,000 training / 1,000 clean test observations, two covariates and an intercept; response replacement shifts the intercept by 6 with independent Gaussian noise. Epsilon additionally includes .05. All 30 replicates remain.
- Real data: cal-housing (20,640 x 8), kin8nm (8,192 x 8), pumadyn-32fh (8,192 x 32), excluding the intercept. Five fixed 80/20 splits, gamma .1, .5, 1, with no injection or .2 Bernoulli response injection. Training-only standardisation is fixed before injection.
- Location/coefficient priors: independent N(0, 10^2); log scale N(0,1); H-B alpha Beta(1,1), with the logit Jacobian. Regression H-B has a .05 standardised-response scale floor; density H-B does not. GammaDiv has the .05 scale floor in its fitting coordinates. Student-t (nu=4), NLL, and downstream NLL have no imposed scale floor.
- H-B temperature: sample covariance determinant^(gamma/2) in density; observed training-response variance^(gamma/2) in conditional regression, divisor n-1. Covariates do not enter a joint-density determinant. GammaDiv uses its recorded monotone density-power target and multiplier 1.
- Four chains. Nominal production settings: 4,000 warm-up / 4,000 retained, NUTS target acceptance .99 and initial step .1. Fixed adopted repairs can retain 8,000. All retained states remain in an adopted fit.
- Required parameter diagnostics: rank-normalised R-hat <= 1.01 and bulk/tail ESS >= 400. New campaigns must meet these criteria on their own results; no stored numerical outcome is used as an acceptance condition.
- FoD/training rankings use the recorded fixed-seed subset of at most 400 draws. Regression epsilon summaries use all retained draws; the saved density epsilon summaries use the scoring subset. New representative posterior files preserve this distinction.
- Cleaning uses integer count posterior quantiles/rounding and the plug-in Gaussian likelihood at the posterior mean parameters, not FoD averages. Oracle cleaning matches the central DPD budget and may remove non-injected rows after exhausting injected rows.
- Test MC-NLPD averages Gaussian densities over all retained draws with log-sum-exp, including for parameters fitted by Student-t. It is a common Gaussian working-density score; Student-t training rankings use its Student-t density.
- Mean and sample SD are across the complete planned dataset/split set. Audit credible endpoints are averaged separately, not pooled into a single posterior interval. Positive real-data audit fractions are model-relative, not known contamination rates.
- The cal-housing common-four-split analysis is exploratory and post hoc: the same influential split is omitted from every method, with all observations retained within each remaining split. The all-five-split primary results are never replaced.
- Best displayed predictive means, AUPRC, and removal F1 are highlighted under the table's comparison scope. Displayed ties are all bold. Label-informed oracle rows are references excluded from practical cleaning emphasis. Counts, audit fractions, interval endpoints, widths, coverage, and variance components are not given a misleading monotone ranking.
